TUTORIAL OVERVIEW

Abstract

Modern computer vision and machine learning systems are routinely deployed in high-stakes settings such as medical image diagnosis, autonomous perception, and remote sensing, where a single point prediction offers no guarantee about how much to trust it. Standard remedies like Bayesian posteriors, bootstrap intervals, softmax confidence scores, and quantile regression either rely on strong distributional assumptions, require expensive re-sampling, or provide no rigorous coverage guarantee at all. Conformal Prediction (CP) closes this gap: it is a model-agnostic, distribution-free framework that wraps around any pre-trained predictor and converts its point predictions into prediction sets or intervals that provably contain the true outcome with a user-specified probability, in finite samples, with no assumptions on the underlying data-generating distribution.

This tutorial gives a self-contained introduction to conformal prediction and a curated tour of selected recent topics of interest to the ICVGIP audience: conformalized image classification with adaptive prediction sets, conformal prediction under distribution shift, and conformal uncertainty quantification for sequential and dependent data. The session closes with a hands-on implementation block so that participants leave able to conformalize their own models.

TUTORIAL OUTLINE

Agenda

I

Uncertainty Quantification & the Foundations of Conformal Prediction

60 min

Motivation for uncertainty quantification in modern AI; overview of existing approaches and their limitations; challenges in obtaining reliable distribution-free guarantees; and an intuitive introduction to Conformal Prediction.

II

Conformal Prediction — Theory, Applications & Advances

60 min

Fundamental principles and theoretical guarantees; practical construction of conformal methods; applications to predictive tasks; recent developments; and practical considerations, limitations, and open research directions.

III

Hands-on Implementation

60 min

Practical implementation of Conformal Prediction for representative prediction tasks; conformalization of pre-trained machine-learning and computer-vision models; and end-to-end workflows for calibration, uncertainty quantification, and evaluation.
