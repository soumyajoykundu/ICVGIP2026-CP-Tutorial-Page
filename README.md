# ICVGIP 2026 Tutorial

## Uncertainty Quantification via Conformal Prediction

**December 21, 2026 · Kolkata, India**

Welcome to the official webpage of the ICVGIP 2026 tutorial **“Uncertainty Quantification via Conformal Prediction.”**

This tutorial introduces the principles of **distribution-free uncertainty quantification** and **conformal prediction**, with an emphasis on how these methods can be used to make modern AI and machine learning systems more reliable and informative.

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

The tutorial will provide an introduction to uncertainty quantification and conformal prediction, covering the key ideas needed to understand and apply distribution-free predictive uncertainty methods.

Participants will gain an understanding of:

- Why uncertainty quantification is important for modern AI systems
- The foundations of distribution-free uncertainty quantification
- The basic principles behind conformal prediction
- How conformal methods provide predictive sets with statistical guarantees
- Applications of conformal prediction in modern machine learning
- Practical considerations when applying conformal prediction to real-world problems
